## Acknowledgement of scope

I understand that Foundation Repair Advocate ("FRA") provides independent floor elevation surveys, on-site observation, and plain-language discussion based on field experience.

**FRA does not:**
- Provide stamped Arizona professional engineering (PE) reports or designs
- Perform a licensed home inspection or certify a property for sale
- Sell, install, or bid foundation repair products (piers, foam, etc.)
- Investigate soil conditions below grade ("see into the dirt")

**FRA findings are based on:**
- Accessible floor elevations and visible conditions
- Surface drainage and related observation
- Professional experience in foundation repair / related field work

I understand that this work is **informational and educational**. It does not guarantee subsurface conditions, future movement, or the outcome of any repair. Recommendations are guidance for decision-making — not a directive to repair or not repair, and not a substitute for engineering, legal advice, or contractor bids.

If I am involved in a real estate transaction, I understand FRA's materials are not a seller disclosure, lender certification, or proof that the foundation is sound.

I agree to seek a licensed Arizona PE, geotechnical firm, attorney, or contractor when that role is required, and I understand FRA will say so when they believe that is needed.
